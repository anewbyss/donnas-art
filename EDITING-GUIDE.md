# How to update your art website (no coding needed)

Everything is edited through a free, point-and-click editor called **Pages CMS**.
When you click **Save**, the website updates itself within about 2 minutes.

## Signing in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** (use the GitHub account that owns this website).
3. Choose the **donnas-art** project.

On the left you'll see four sections: **Artwork**, **Collections**, **Pages** and **Site settings**.

---

## Posting a new piece

1. Click **Artwork → Add an entry**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** – the name of the piece.
   - **Main image** – click to upload a photo straight from your computer or phone.
   - **Extra images** – optional close-ups or "on the wall" photos.
   - **Collections** – tick every style, era and theme it belongs to.
   - **Availability** – Available, Prints available, Price on request, Sold, or Not for sale.
   - **Price** – numbers only (e.g. `450`). Leave blank to hide the price.
   - **Etsy listing link** – paste the Etsy page address to show a **Buy on Etsy** button.
   - **Year, Medium, Size, Description** – anything you'd like visitors to know.
   - **Feature on the home page** – switch on for your favourite pieces.
   - **Date posted** – newest dates appear first in the gallery.
3. Click **Save**. That's it!

**Tips**
- Photograph art in daylight, straight-on, and crop out the background.
- Not ready to show something yet? Switch on **Hide from the website (draft)**.
- When a piece sells, just change **Availability** to **Sold**. It stays in your portfolio
  with a "Request something similar" button.

## How visitors can buy

| You set… | Visitors see… |
|---|---|
| An **Etsy listing link** | a **Buy on Etsy** button |
| A **Price** + your **PayPal email** (in Site settings) | a **Buy with PayPal** button that takes them to PayPal checkout |
| Both | both buttons — they choose |
| Neither | an **Ask about this work** button that goes to your contact form |

PayPal sends you an email for every sale, including the buyer's shipping address.

## Collections (styles, eras, themes)

On the website these are called **Arcana**. In the editor they're under **Collections**.

Collections organise your gallery. Each one gets its own page you can share,
and appears as a filter button on the Gallery page.

- **Collections → Add an entry**, give it a **Name**, and pick whether it's a
  **Style** (Abstract, Portraits…), an **Era** (1990s, Recent Work…) or a **Theme** (Florals, By the Sea…).
- **Display order**: lower numbers show first.
- If you rename a collection, open the artwork in it and re-tick the new name.

## About & Contact pages

**Pages → About** – change your bio and photo.
**Pages → Contact** – change the intro text above the contact form.

## Site settings

Your name, home-page headline and picture, email address, commission status,
social-media links and PayPal details all live in **Site settings**.

- **Social media links**: paste full addresses like `https://instagram.com/yourname`.
  Empty ones are hidden automatically.
- **Accepting commissions**: switch off to hide the "Request art" buttons.
- **Contact form address**: by default the contact form opens the visitor's email app.
  For messages to arrive straight in your inbox, create a free form at
  **https://formspree.io**, copy its link (looks like `https://formspree.io/f/abcd1234`)
  and paste it here.

## Sharing your work

Every artwork and collection page has **Share** buttons (Facebook, Pinterest, X,
WhatsApp, email, copy link). Links shared on social media show a preview with the image.
Fans can also follow new work through the RSS link at the bottom of every page.

## How your art is protected

These protections are automatic. You don't need to do anything.

- **Your original photos are never published.** Visitors only get resized copies
  (no bigger than 1600 pixels), which look sharp on screen but are too small to print well.
- **Hidden photo details are removed.** Phone photos can contain the location where they
  were taken. That information is removed from every image on the site.
- **Saving is discouraged.** Right-clicking, dragging and long-pressing images is turned off.
  (Nobody can stop screenshots, but this stops casual copying.)
- **AI companies are told no.** The site asks AI crawlers (OpenAI, Google's AI, Anthropic,
  Meta, Common Crawl and others) not to use your work for training. Reputable companies
  follow this request. Normal Google search still works, so people can find you.
- **Copyright notice** in the footer of every page.
- **Spam protection** on the contact form, and your email address is hidden from spam robots.
- **Security settings** that stop other websites from embedding your pages or adding code to them.

The most important protection is your **GitHub account**: anyone who gets into it can change
your site. Turn on two-factor authentication at https://github.com/settings/security

## Something look wrong?

Every save is kept in history, so nothing is ever lost. In GitHub, open the
**Actions** tab to see whether the latest update published successfully (green tick).
